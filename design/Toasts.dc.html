Rules
One at a time — a new toast replaces the old rather than stacking.
Destructive actions get Undo, not a confirmation dialog.
Never covers the primary action of the screen beneath.
4 seconds by default, 6 when there is an action to take.
Sits 16pt above the safe area, or above a sticky bar when one is present.
